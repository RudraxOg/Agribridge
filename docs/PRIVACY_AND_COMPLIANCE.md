# Privacy and compliance

The retired registry subsystem stores no personal identity, land, biometric or payout-account records. The remaining trade application prohibits card numbers, CVV and bank credentials in application data, fixtures, logs and browser storage.

Sensitive files are private and accessed with authenticated short-lived signed URLs. Service-role, payment, LiveKit, BHASHINI and cron secrets are server-only. The service worker excludes payments, API payloads and signed documents.

Before production, counsel and regulated providers must review consent language, lawful purpose, retention/deletion schedules, data-principal rights, grievance handling, payment-flow terminology, settlement contracts, marketplace liabilities and cross-border/export data needs. The prototype is a technical control baseline, not legal certification.
