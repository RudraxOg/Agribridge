# Privacy and compliance

The schema and UI prohibit raw fingerprints, facial templates, complete Aadhaar values, card numbers, CVV and bank credentials. Identity records keep provider, verification reference, masked identifier, optional last four digits, status, timestamp and consent link. Alternatives to biometric verification are offered and recorded.

Sensitive files are private and accessed with authenticated short-lived signed URLs. Service-role, payment, LiveKit, BHASHINI and cron secrets are server-only. The service worker excludes identity, payments, API payloads and signed documents.

Before production, counsel and regulated providers must review consent language, lawful purpose, retention/deletion schedules, data-principal rights, grievance handling, Aadhaar offline-verification use, payment-flow terminology, settlement contracts, marketplace liabilities and cross-border/export data needs. The prototype is a technical control baseline, not legal certification.
